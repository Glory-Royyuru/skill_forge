# Customer Refund Policy

We want every customer to feel confident shopping with us. This page explains how refunds work.

## Standard Returns

Most items can be returned for a refund within 30 days of delivery. If your item arrived
damaged or defective, we understand these things take longer to notice or arrange, so we
extend that window to 60 days from delivery instead.

## VIP Members

VIP members receive an additional 15 days on top of whichever return window otherwise
applies to their order, standard or damaged.

## Final Sale Items

Final sale items are not eligible for return or refund. The one exception is damage: if a
final sale item arrives damaged, you have 7 days from delivery to report it and receive a
refund. After that window, even a damaged final sale item cannot be refunded.

## Opened Electronics

Electronics that have been opened but are not damaged or defective can still be refunded,
at 85% of the purchase price, to cover restocking and inspection costs. Damaged electronics
are refunded in full, opened or not.

## Gift Orders

Orders placed and marked as a gift are refunded as store credit rather than back to the
original payment method.

## High-Value Refunds

Any refund whose value, after any adjustments described above, exceeds $500 requires a
manager review before it can be issued. These requests are escalated rather than processed
automatically.

## Already-Refunded Orders

An order that has already been refunded cannot be refunded a second time.

## Delivery Records

Return and refund windows are always measured against our system's delivery record for an
order, not a customer's recollection of when an item arrived. If there's ever a discrepancy,
the system record is what we go by.

## Frequent Refunds

Customers who have received 3 or more refunds within the last 90 days will have any new
refund request reviewed by a manager before it is processed, rather than being handled
automatically.
